# Blessings Wallet

**Blessings Wallet** is a faith-centered Android application created to help users recognize, record, and reflect on the financial blessings they receive while intentionally preparing their tithes, offerings, and other purposeful funds.

Unlike traditional budgeting applications, Blessings Wallet does **not track expenses**. Its purpose is to help users focus on what they have received, see how much has been allocated for stewardship, and reflect on God's provision throughout the day, week, month, and year.

## Purpose

There are times when we may feel that God has not provided enough or that our prayers have not been answered. Yet when we look back at everything we have received, we can often see how faithfully God has provided for us.

Blessings Wallet was created to make that reflection easier.

The app encourages users to:

* recognize the financial blessings they receive;
* faithfully prepare their tithes and offerings;
* intentionally allocate funds for meaningful purposes;
* develop better stewardship habits;
* reflect on God's provision over time; and
* cultivate a heart of gratitude.

## Main Features

### Record Blessings

Users can manually record financial blessings such as:

* Salary
* Business income
* Freelance income
* Bonuses
* Gifts
* Allowances
* Commissions
* Honoraria
* Other financial blessings

Each blessing can include an amount, category, and date.

### Blessing Categories

Users can organize their blessings using customizable categories.

Default or commonly used categories may include:

* Salary
* Business
* Freelance
* Gift
* Bonus
* Allowance
* Other

Users may create, edit, or remove categories depending on their needs.

### Tithes

Blessings Wallet can automatically calculate the intended tithe from recorded blessings.

The default tithe percentage may be set to **10%**, while users remain responsible for choosing settings according to their personal convictions and stewardship practices.

### Offerings

Users can prepare and monitor their intended offerings separately from their tithe.

Offerings may be configured according to the user's preferred amount or percentage.

### Custom Funds

Users can create additional purposeful funds such as:

* Mission Fund
* Church Building Fund
* Savings
* Emergency Fund
* Family Fund
* Education Fund
* Ministry Fund
* Other personal funds

Each fund can have its own allocation percentage or amount.

### Fund Tracking

Blessings Wallet helps users distinguish between amounts that have been:

* Allocated
* Prepared
* Given
* Released
* Unallocated

Actions such as **Mark as Given** or **Release Fund** only update the user's personal records.

The application does not transfer real money.

### Blessing Summary

Users can review their blessings according to different periods:

* Today
* Week
* Month
* Year

This allows users to look back and recognize how much they have received over time.

### Unallocated Blessings

Blessings Wallet may show the amount of blessings that have not been allocated to tithes, offerings, or custom funds.

This amount should not be interpreted as the user's actual remaining cash or bank balance because Blessings Wallet does not record expenses.

### Simple Reminders

Users may enable a reminder and select a preferred:

* Day
* Time

The reminder can help users review their blessings and prepare their intended tithes and offerings.

### Local-First Privacy

Blessings Wallet is designed primarily as an **offline, local-first application**.

The app does not require:

* An online account
* Bank account access
* GCash account access
* Credit or debit card information
* Cloud synchronization
* Expense records
* Advertising
* Location tracking

Blessing, tithe, offering, and fund records are intended to remain locally on the user's device.

## What Blessings Wallet Is Not

Despite the word **Wallet** in its name, Blessings Wallet is not a digital financial wallet.

The application does not:

* hold real money;
* send or receive money;
* transfer tithes or offerings;
* connect to bank accounts;
* connect to electronic wallets;
* process payments;
* provide loans;
* provide investments;
* track everyday expenses; or
* provide professional financial advice.

Blessings Wallet is a **personal stewardship and record-keeping application**.

## Faith and Stewardship

Blessings Wallet is inspired by Christian and Seventh-day Adventist principles of stewardship, gratitude, faithfulness, and returning to God what belongs to Him.

The application is intended to encourage users to remember that every blessing is an opportunity to recognize God's provision.

> **Every blessing counted is a reminder of God's provision.**

## About the Developers

Blessings Wallet was created by a young married Seventh-day Adventist IT couple who are passionate about using technology to serve God and bless others.

The app grew from a desire to help friends, family, and fellow believers prepare their tithes and offerings while becoming more aware of the many ways God has provided throughout their lives.

Blessings Wallet is designed to keep stewardship records local to the user's device.

The application does not require an online account and does not automatically transmit users' blessing, tithe, offering, or custom-fund records to the developers.

Users should review the official **Privacy Policy** and **Data Safety** information provided with the application for complete details.

## Voluntary Support

Blessings Wallet is intended to be available without requiring payment for its primary features.

Users may be provided with an optional way to voluntarily support the continued development of the application.

Voluntary support does not unlock premium features, special privileges, or additional digital benefits.

## Platform

Blessings Wallet is developed for **Android devices**.

The goal of Blessings Wallet is not simply to help users count money.

It is to help them **count their blessings**.

By looking back at what they have received, users can better appreciate God's provision, become more intentional with their stewardship, and develop a deeper attitude of gratitude.
