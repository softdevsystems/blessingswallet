1. About Blessings Wallet

Blessings Wallet is a faith-centered personal stewardship application designed to help users record financial blessings they receive and intentionally allocate portions toward tithes, offerings, and user-created funds. It encourages gratitude, responsible stewardship, and reflection on God's provision.

Blessings Wallet is not an expense-tracking application and does not attempt to determine where a user's money has been spent or calculate an actual cash, bank, or financial balance.

2. Not a Bank or Digital Wallet

Despite the word “Wallet” in its name, Blessings Wallet is not a bank, electronic wallet, payment processor, remittance service, investment platform, lending service, or other financial institution.
