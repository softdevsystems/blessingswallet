Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 16, 2026 · Last updated: August 16, 2026

Blessings Wallet respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Blessings Wallet and describes the privacy controls available to you.

1. Overview

Blessings Wallet is designed primarily as an offline, local-first application. The application helps users manually record financial blessings they receive and calculate or allocate amounts for tithes, offerings, and other user-created funds. Blessings Wallet is designed so that the user's stewardship records remain locally on the user's device.

2. Information You May Enter

Depending on the features you use, Blessings Wallet may allow you to enter:

  • amounts of blessings received;
  • blessing categories such as salary, business, freelance, gifts, bonuses, or other categories;
  • dates and optional descriptions or notes;
  • tithe percentages and offering settings;
  • custom fund names and fund allocation percentages or amounts;
  • records showing that a fund was marked as given or released;
  • reminder day and time;
  • currency preferences; and
  • other application settings.

These records are used only to provide the application's blessing-tracking, stewardship, calculation, fund-allocation, and reminder features.

3. Local Processing

Blessings Wallet is designed to process your blessing and fund information locally on your device. The developers do not operate a Blessings Wallet server for receiving or storing your personal blessing records in the version of the application covered by this Privacy Policy. Your locally entered financial records are therefore not automatically transmitted to the developers.

4. No User Account Required

Blessings Wallet does not require users to create an online account. We do not require a Blessings Wallet username, password, phone number, or email address to use the application's primary features.

5. Information We Do Not Request for Core Functionality

Blessings Wallet does not require access to your bank account, debit or credit card, GCash balance, electronic-wallet balance, investment account, credit information, SMS messages, call logs, contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, or browsing history for its primary blessing-tracking functionality.

Blessings Wallet does not automatically retrieve your salary, income, banking transactions, purchases, or expenses.

6. Blessings Wallet Does Not Track Expenses

Blessings Wallet intentionally does not maintain a record of users' everyday expenses. Amounts entered into the application represent blessings received and allocations made by the user.

An amount displayed as Unallocated Blessings or similar wording does not represent the user's actual bank balance, cash balance, or total remaining money. Blessings Wallet cannot determine how much money a user actually has because the application does not record personal spending or expenses.

7. Notifications and Reminders

Blessings Wallet may allow users to enable reminders for reviewing their blessings or preparing their tithes and offerings. If enabled, the app may request Android notification permission.

Reminder settings may include whether reminders are enabled, the reminder day, and the reminder time. These settings are stored locally and are used only to provide the reminder feature. You may disable reminders within Blessings Wallet or revoke notification permission through your Android device settings.

8. Sharing the App

The Share this App feature is intended only to allow users to share information about Blessings Wallet, such as its Google Play listing or application information. It does not intentionally include your personal blessing, tithe, offering, or fund records.

Any sharing action is initiated by the user through applications or services available on the user's device. Those external applications or services may have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

9. Voluntary Support and GCash

Blessings Wallet may display information that allows users to voluntarily support the developers and continued development of the application. Providing support is entirely optional and is not required to use Blessings Wallet.

Blessings Wallet itself does not process those payments and does not request or store your GCash PIN, GCash password, wallet balance, authentication code, or other GCash login credentials.

If you voluntarily send support through GCash or another external payment service, the transaction takes place outside Blessings Wallet and is handled by that third-party provider under its own terms and privacy practices. Voluntary support does not provide additional features, premium access, special privileges, or other digital benefits within Blessings Wallet.

10. Contacting the Developers

If you voluntarily contact us through email or another communication service, we may receive information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, message, screenshots, device information you voluntarily provide, or other information contained in your communication.

We use this information only as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, investigate a reported problem, provide technical assistance, improve Blessings Wallet, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

11. Advertising

The version of Blessings Wallet covered by this Privacy Policy is intended to operate without third-party advertising. We do not use or sell your Blessings Wallet financial records for targeted advertising.

12. Analytics and Tracking

The version of Blessings Wallet covered by this Privacy Policy is designed not to transmit your blessing records to advertising or behavioral analytics services. We do not sell your blessing records, use them for targeted advertising, create advertising profiles from your stewardship information, or intentionally track your financial activity outside Blessings Wallet.

If analytics, crash reporting, cloud synchronization, online accounts, or similar network services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated.

13. Third-Party Libraries and Services

Blessings Wallet may use Android system components and software libraries required for normal operation. We aim to minimize third-party services that receive user information. Future third-party services or SDKs that collect, receive, or process user data will be reviewed and applicable privacy disclosures updated where necessary.

14. Data Retention

Information stored locally on your device generally remains there until you edit a record, delete an individual record, use a Delete All Data feature if available, clear the application's storage or data, uninstall Blessings Wallet, or reset or replace your device.

Because the developers do not maintain a server containing your blessing records, we generally cannot access or remotely delete those local records. Information voluntarily sent to the developers for support may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, investigate, resolve technical problems, maintain appropriate support records, protect the application and its users, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

15. How to Delete Your Local Data

You remain in control of the records stored by Blessings Wallet on your device. You may delete individual records using app controls. If available, Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All Data may be used to permanently remove locally stored Blessings Wallet records after confirmation.

You may also clear the app's stored data through Android application settings or uninstall the application. Because Blessings Wallet does not require an online user account, there is no Blessings Wallet server account that must be separately deleted.

16. Security

Blessings Wallet is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of personal and stewardship information. However, no electronic storage system or device can guarantee absolute security. The security of your local records may depend on your Android device, screen lock, operating system, security updates, whether the device is compromised, other installed applications, and physical access to the device.

17. Children's Privacy

Blessings Wallet is a general faith-based stewardship application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The application's primary blessing and stewardship records are designed to remain locally on the user's device.

18. International Data Transfers

Blessings Wallet does not automatically transmit locally stored blessing, tithe, offering, or custom-fund records to a Blessings Wallet server. Therefore, the developers do not ordinarily transfer these core local records internationally. If you independently use a third-party app or service, information you provide to that third party may be processed according to that provider's own practices.

19. Your Privacy Choices

  • choose whether to enter a blessing;
  • choose categories and amounts;
  • configure tithe, offering, and custom funds;
  • edit or delete records;
  • enable or disable reminders;
  • choose the reminder day and time;
  • grant or revoke notification permission;
  • choose whether to share the app or contact the developers; and
  • delete all locally stored application data.

20. Philippine Data Privacy Principles

Where applicable, we aim to handle personal information consistently with applicable Philippine privacy laws and principles, including transparency, legitimate purpose, proportionality, appropriate security, responsible processing, and respect for individual rights.

21. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when application functionality, data-handling practices, SDKs, laws, regulations, or platform requirements change. The Last Updated date will be revised when material changes are made.
